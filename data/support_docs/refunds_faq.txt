REFUNDS & RETURNS POLICY - FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: What is your refund policy?
A: We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all plans and products. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5-7 business days. For annual subscriptions, we provide a prorated refund for the remaining months if you cancel within 30 days of renewal. After 30 days, subscription refunds are evaluated case-by-case. One-time purchases of physical products follow our returns policy. Digital products and downloadable content are non-refundable once downloaded or accessed unless defective.

Q: How long does a refund take to process?
A: Once your refund request is approved, processing times are: Credit/debit cards take 5-7 business days (depending on your bank). PayPal refunds appear within 1-3 business days. Bank transfers (ACH) take 7-10 business days. Store credit is applied instantly. You will receive an email confirmation when your refund is initiated. If you haven't seen the refund after the stated timeframe, contact your bank before reaching out to us, as processing times can vary.

Q: Can I exchange an item instead of getting a refund?
A: Yes, exchanges are available within 30 days of purchase. For size, color, or variant exchanges of physical products, initiate at Account > Orders > Exchange. We ship the replacement at no cost for domestic orders once we receive the return (standard shipping applies). For international exchanges, you cover return shipping and we cover the outbound replacement. You can also request an exchange for a different product of equal or lesser value, with a store credit issued for any price difference.

Q: I received the wrong item. What do I do?
A: If you received the wrong item, contact support within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a photo of the item received. We will send the correct item with expedited shipping at no charge and provide a prepaid return label for the wrong item. Do not discard the incorrect item until we request its return. We apologize for the inconvenience and will apply a 10% discount code to your account as compensation for the fulfillment error.

Q: My subscription auto-renewed. Can I get a refund?
A: Auto-renewal refund requests are considered if submitted within 7 days of the renewal charge and you have not used the service during the new period. We send renewal reminder emails 14 days and 3 days before your renewal date. If you missed the renewal notification, we will evaluate your request. To prevent future auto-renewals, disable auto-renew under Account > Subscription > Manage. Note: We cannot refund renewal charges if the account was actively used after the renewal date.

Q: How do I return a defective product?
A: Report defective products within 90 days of purchase at Account > Orders > Report Defect. Attach photos or a video demonstrating the defect. We will provide a prepaid return shipping label. Once we inspect the returned item (3-5 business days), we will send a replacement or full refund including original shipping costs. For defects discovered after 90 days, coverage may apply under manufacturer warranty — we can connect you with the manufacturer's warranty team.

Q: Can I return software or digital licenses?
A: Software licenses and digital products are generally non-refundable once the license key is revealed or the download is initiated. Exceptions are made for: (1) products that are defective or don't function as advertised, (2) duplicate purchases made by accident within 24 hours, (3) products purchased for an incompatible system despite meeting published requirements. For exception requests, contact support with detailed explanation. Fraudulent refund requests may result in account suspension.

Q: What items cannot be returned?
A: The following items are final sale and cannot be returned or refunded: Downloadable digital content once accessed, personalized or custom-made items, perishable goods, hazardous materials, gift cards, and items marked "Final Sale" at time of purchase. Subscription services cannot be refunded for past billing periods. API usage charges are non-refundable. For hygiene reasons, certain personal care items cannot be returned once opened. Always check the product page for return eligibility before purchasing.

SHIPPING & DELIVERY - FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: What are the shipping options and costs?
A: We offer Standard Shipping (5-7 business days, free on orders over $50, otherwise $4.99), Expedited Shipping (2-3 business days, $9.99), and Overnight Shipping (next business day, $24.99). Orders placed before 2 PM EST ship same day on business days. Saturday delivery is available in select areas for an additional $5. International shipping rates vary by destination — see our shipping calculator at checkout.

Q: How do I track my order?
A: Once your order ships, you will receive a tracking email with a tracking number and a direct link to the carrier's tracking page. You can also track orders by logging in and going to Account > Orders > Track. We ship via UPS, FedEx, and USPS depending on your location and shipping method. Tracking updates may take 24 hours to appear after the shipping confirmation email is sent.

Q: My package is delayed. What should I do?
A: First check the tracking page for the latest status. Carrier delays of 1-2 days are common during peak seasons (holidays, major sales events). If the tracking shows "in transit" for more than 5 business days without updates, contact us with your order number. If tracking shows "delivered" but you haven't received the package, check with neighbors and building management first. We will file a carrier claim and reship or refund if the package is confirmed lost.

Q: Do you ship internationally?
A: Yes, we ship to over 50 countries. International orders typically arrive in 7-14 business days via standard international shipping. Expedited international shipping (3-5 business days) is available for most major countries. Import duties, customs fees, and local taxes are the recipient's responsibility and are not included in our shipping charges. Some restricted products cannot be shipped internationally — see our restricted items list.

Q: Can I change my shipping address after ordering?
A: Shipping address changes are possible within 1 hour of placing an order before it enters the fulfillment queue. Contact support immediately with your order number and correct address. Once an order is packed or shipped, address changes are not possible. You would need to contact the carrier directly to redirect the package (additional fees may apply). For future orders, you can save multiple addresses in Account > Saved Addresses.

Q: What if my item arrives damaged?
A: If your item arrives damaged, take photos of the packaging and the damaged item before discarding anything. Report the damage within 48 hours of delivery via Account > Orders > Report Issue or contact support with your photos and order number. For minor damage, we may offer a partial refund. For significant damage, we will send a prepaid return label and reship or refund. Do not use or install a damaged product before contacting us.

Q: How do I return an item?
A: Returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery for items in original condition. Initiate a return at Account > Orders > Return Item. Select your return reason and preferred resolution (refund or exchange). We will email a prepaid return shipping label for defective items. For buyer's remorse returns, return shipping cost is deducted from the refund. Once we receive and inspect the return (2-3 business days), we process your refund or send the exchange.

Q: Can I pick up my order locally?
A: Local pickup is available at select warehouse locations. During checkout, if pickup is available in your area, the option will appear under shipping methods. Pickup orders are typically ready within 2-4 hours on business days. You will receive an email when your order is ready. Bring your order confirmation email and a valid photo ID. Someone else can pick up with written authorization and their own ID.

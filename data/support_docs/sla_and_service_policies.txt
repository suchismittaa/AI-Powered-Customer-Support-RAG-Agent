PART A: UPTIME AND AVAILABILITY GUARANTEES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

A.1 UPTIME COMMITMENTS BY PLAN

Starter Plan:    99.5% monthly uptime (allows ~3.6 hours downtime/month)
Growth Plan:     99.7% monthly uptime (allows ~2.2 hours downtime/month)
Business Plan:   99.9% monthly uptime (allows ~43 minutes downtime/month)
Enterprise Plan: 99.95% monthly uptime (custom — allows ~22 minutes downtime/month)

A.2 UPTIME CALCULATION
Uptime % = ((Total minutes in month - Downtime minutes) / Total minutes in month) × 100
Downtime is counted from the moment an incident is declared on status.company.com to the moment it is resolved. Planned maintenance windows do not count as downtime when communicated 72 hours in advance.

A.3 SLA CREDITS
If uptime falls below guaranteed levels, customers receive account credits:
- Downtime 0.1% - 1.0% below SLA: 10% credit of monthly fee
- Downtime 1.1% - 5.0% below SLA: 25% credit of monthly fee
- Downtime 5.1%+ below SLA: 50% credit of monthly fee
Credits are applied automatically to the next invoice. Credits do not apply to: force majeure events, customer-caused outages, scheduled maintenance, or third-party service failures outside our control (e.g., AWS outages). Maximum credit is 50% of monthly fee regardless of downtime duration.

A.4 CLAIMING SLA CREDITS
Credits are assessed and applied automatically for Business and Enterprise plans. Starter and Growth plan customers must submit a credit request within 30 days of the incident via the support portal. Include the incident date/time and affected services.

- Hours: Monday-Friday, 9AM-5PM EST
- Severity levels: Standard only

GROWTH PLAN SUPPORT:
- Channels: Email + Live Chat
- First response (email): 12 business hours
- First response (chat): 2 business hours during business hours
- Resolution target: 3 business days
- Hours: Monday-Friday, 8AM-8PM EST
- Severity levels: Standard + High

BUSINESS PLAN SUPPORT:
- Channels: Email + Live Chat + Phone
- First response (email): 4 hours (24/7)
- First response (chat): 30 minutes (24/7)
- First response (phone): Immediate during business hours, callback within 2 hours after hours
- Resolution target: 24 hours for critical, 2 business days for standard
- Hours: 24/7
- Severity levels: Critical + High + Standard

ENTERPRISE PLAN SUPPORT:
- Channels: Dedicated Slack channel + Email + Phone + Video call
- First response: 1 hour (24/7) for critical, 4 hours for high, 8 hours for standard
- Resolution target: 4 hours for critical, 24 hours for high, 3 business days for standard
- Hours: 24/7/365

- Quarterly Business Reviews included

B.2 SEVERITY LEVEL DEFINITIONS

CRITICAL (P1): Complete service unavailability, data loss, security breach, or payment system failure affecting all users. All hands response. Status page updated every 30 minutes.

HIGH (P2): Major feature unavailable, significant performance degradation affecting many users, integration failures affecting business operations.

STANDARD (P3): Non-critical bugs, feature questions, billing inquiries, account configuration questions.

LOW (P4): Feature requests, general feedback, documentation questions. No SLA commitment.

PART C: DATA RETENTION AND BACKUP POLICIES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

C.1 DATA BACKUP SCHEDULE
- Continuous real-time replication to secondary region
- Full snapshots: Daily (retained 30 days)
- Weekly snapshots: Retained 3 months
- Monthly snapshots: Retained 1 year
- Annual snapshots: Retained 7 years (billing records)
All backups are encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3).

C.2 DATA RETENTION AFTER ACCOUNT CLOSURE
- Active accounts: Data retained indefinitely while subscription active
- Cancelled accounts (within 90 days): Full data preserved, account can be reactivated
- Cancelled accounts (after 90 days): All user data deleted permanently
- Exception: Billing records retained 7 years per financial regulations
- Exception: Legal hold data retained per court order or regulatory requirement

C.3 DATA RESIDENCY OPTIONS
Default: US-East (Virginia, AWS)
Available regions (Enterprise plan only): EU-West (Ireland), EU-Central (Frankfurt), AP-Southeast (Singapore), US-West (Oregon)
Region selection at account creation only — migration between regions requires Enterprise support engagement and typically takes 5-10 business days.

C.4 DISASTER RECOVERY
Recovery Time Objective (RTO): 4 hours (Business), 1 hour (Enterprise)
Recovery Point Objective (RPO): 1 hour (Business), 15 minutes (Enterprise)
Annual DR drills conducted and results published in security trust center.

PART D: SECURITY AND COMPLIANCE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

D.1 CERTIFICATIONS AND COMPLIANCE
- SOC 2 Type II: Annual audit, report available under NDA to Enterprise customers
- ISO 27001: Certified, certificate available on request
- GDPR: Compliant, DPA available for EU customers
- HIPAA: BAA available for Enterprise customers (healthcare)
- PCI-DSS Level 1: For payment processing components
- CCPA: Compliant for California residents

D.2 PENETRATION TESTING
Annual third-party penetration tests conducted. Summaries available to Enterprise customers under NDA. Bug bounty program available at bugbounty.company.com — rewards up to $10,000 for critical vulnerabilities.

D.3 SECURITY INCIDENT RESPONSE
Confirmed security incidents are communicated to affected customers within 72 hours (GDPR requirement). Our security team is reachable 24/7 at security@company.com. Customers suspected of being affected by a breach receive personal notification via their registered email.

D.4 VULNERABILITY DISCLOSURE
We follow responsible disclosure principles. If you discover a security vulnerability, report it to security@company.com before public disclosure. We commit to responding within 48 hours and resolving critical issues within 30 days.
